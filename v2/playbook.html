Short notes on what I’ve learned.

Things I keep coming back to, on leading design, building teams, and shipping. Newest first. Add to it whenever something sticks.

1
September 2026

Make the work visible before it’s ready.

The earlier people see a rough version, the earlier they can help. Polish invites approval; sketches invite thinking. I’ve never regretted sharing too soon.

2
August 2026

Clarity is a kindness.

A clear bar, a clear decision, a clear no. People can work with any of those. What they can’t work with is ambiguity dressed up as flexibility.

3
July 2026

Systems outlast heroics.

Design systems, team rituals, habits. Every time I’ve solved a problem with a system instead of a person, it stayed solved.
