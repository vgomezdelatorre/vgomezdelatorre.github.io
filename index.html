Leadership Style

My leadership style is a blend of servant + player-coach.

I call it Enablement Leadership.
It combines support with accountability and reflects an active role in creating the conditions for success.

I enable designers to think critically, collaborate effectively across disciplines, navigate constraints with confidence, and grow their craft while delivering meaningful outcomes. I enable product managers by grounding decisions in strong research and shared priorities. I enable marketing partners by aligning the in-product experience with external messaging so the story feels cohesive. I enable engineering by making the "why" behind design decisions visible and inviting their ideas early, so direction is shaped together rather than handed down.

I enable how people, process, and product interact and make decisions, building trust along the way. With trust in place, we align on today's priorities while shaping strategy for what needs to scale tomorrow.

If you enable people to do what they do best. Trust grows. Great work follows.
