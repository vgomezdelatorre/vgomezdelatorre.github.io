I'm a design leader with experience spanning product design, design systems, and cross-functional strategy. My work lives at the intersection of craft and clarity — I care equally about the quality of the pixels and the quality of the decisions that produced them.

I've led teams across early-stage startups and scaled organizations, and I've found that the fundamentals are consistent: build trust, make the work visible, and create the conditions for people to do their best thinking.

Open to new opportunities. I'm looking for roles where design has a seat at the table, where there's genuine investment in craft, and where I can help shape both the product and the team building it.

I'm particularly drawn to companies working on complex, meaningful problems — products where the design decisions genuinely matter to the people using them.

I think a lot about systems — design systems, team systems, habit systems. I'm an avid reader, a slow runner, and someone who believes that the best ideas often come from the least expected places.

Have something in mind? I'd love to hear from you.
